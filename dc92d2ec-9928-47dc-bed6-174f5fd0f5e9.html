Development notes

Hearth & Heim is an actively developed survival prototype built in Godot, with a Blender-based asset workflow. Current systems include resource gathering, building, farming, crafting, campfire cooking, save/load, and nighttime wolf encounters.

Story foundations and the first exploration landmarks are implemented. Further narrative encounters, region mechanics, balancing, and audiovisual polish remain in development. No public release date has been announced.
